# Nursing Resources

Free, reusable educational resources for nursing students and educators.

## Universal Nursing Concept Map

[Download the blank concept-map worksheet](./Universal_Nursing_Concept_Map.pdf).

The worksheet provides space to organize:

- core disease or condition concepts;
- pathophysiology, causes, and risk factors;
- signs, symptoms, labs, and diagnostics;
- nursing assessments, priorities, and interventions;
- treatment, medication, patient teaching, and complications; and
- clinical or exam connections.

It is designed as a general study aid and can be printed or completed digitally with a PDF annotation tool.

## Important note

This resource is for education and study. It is not medical advice and does not replace clinical judgment, institutional policy, or guidance from a qualified instructor or healthcare professional. Do not enter patient-identifying information into copies you share publicly.
